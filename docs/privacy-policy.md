# Vaporlens privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Vaporlens is an unofficial Steam client for iOS and Android, with game summaries from VaporLens
(vaporlens.app). It is not affiliated with Valve Corporation. This policy covers the app and the
parts of vaporlens.app the app uses.

## Steam

You sign in to Steam directly. Your password, messages, photos, voice and everything else you do
in Steam through the app goes straight to Steam's servers, as it does in Steam's own apps. We
never receive your password, your Steam login token, your messages or your calls. Valve's
privacy policy covers what Steam does with them:
https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

## VaporLens

When you sign in to Steam, the app also signs you in to vaporlens.app with "Sign in through
Steam". vaporlens.app receives your SteamID from Steam's signed sign-in answer, not your Steam
login token.

vaporlens.app keeps:

- your SteamID, to know who you are;
- your Steam library and wishlist, if you import them in Settings → VaporLens. vaporlens.app
  fetches them from Steam's public profile data, and uses them to work out how well a game fits
  what you play;
- the games you ask to have analyzed.

We use this only to run those features. We don't sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it
for ads.

## On your device

Your Steam login and VaporLens session are kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS,
Keystore-backed storage on Android). The app caches lists and pages on the device to show them
faster, and keeps your settings there. Signing out deletes the cache and that account's logins.

Notifications for messages and calls are created on the device. No push service is involved.

The app asks for the microphone (voice chat), the camera and your photos (sending pictures in
chats) and notifications, only when you use those features.

## What we don't do

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

Signing out of every account removes everything the app stored on your device. To delete what
vaporlens.app keeps about you, [how to request deletion].

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
