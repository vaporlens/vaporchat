# Changelog

## 0.6 (2026-10-17)

- Get alerts for messages and calls while the app is in the background, and choose per chat which
  ones alert. On Android, "Stay connected" in the new Settings screen keeps them coming.
- Ring on incoming calls, with a short beep instead while you're in voice.
- Add Steam's notification inbox behind a bell.
- Keep unread messages in sync with Steam, mark chats read on your other devices too, and show a
  dot on the Chat tab while anything is unread.
- Show what friends are playing and when they're away, and set your own status in Settings.
- Open links in messages, react to messages and see when friends are typing.
- Switch between a group's rooms and see its members. Muted rooms no longer show as unread.
- Wishlist, follow and ignore games from their store page.
- Show a game's editions, DLC, system requirements, Early Access notes and similar games.
- Show playtime and when you last played on My games cards.
- Fix voice not playing through Bluetooth headsets on Android.

## 0.5 (2026-10-15)

- Split the game page into Overview, Media, About, Reviews and News tabs.
- Show a game's Steam reviews, most helpful first, and its news, updates and events.
- List a game's features, like single-player or controller support, on its page.
- Show "Coming soon" with the release date or window for games that aren't out yet.

## 0.4.3 (2026-10-15)

- Fix Steam Guard login failing on Android when approving in the Steam app on the same phone.

## 0.4.2 (2026-10-15)

- Keep the search bar hidden while lists load and when switching sub-tabs.

## 0.4.1 (2026-10-14)

- Fix missing images for newer games in the My games list.

## 0.4 (2026-10-14)

- Use the app without signing in: it opens in the store, and account lists and chat offer to sign in.
- Add a My games list to the Store tab, with recently played games first.
- Add a pull-down search bar to the store and release lists. It searches the whole store, or filters
  the wishlist and library by name.
- Mark owned and wishlisted games on store cards and game pages.
- Use native screen transitions, swipe-back and back handling.
- Make the app smoother on older Android phones.
- Fix the Android package ID for Google Play.

## 0.3 (2026-10-08)

- Add Android support: background voice calls, back navigation, zoom, icons and signed release builds.
- Load more of every store, release and calendar list, and older chat messages, while scrolling.
- Go back from pushed screens by swiping from the left edge on iOS.

## 0.2 (2026-10-07)

- Open on cached data and refresh it from Steam in the background.
- Show store games in a two-column grid with prices on the art.
- Redesign the game page after VaporLens's own report layout.
- Play game trailers fullscreen and list all media in a two-column page.
- Stop requesting own and friends' status, which signed other Steam clients out of chat and emptied
  their friends lists.

## 0.1 (2026-10-06)

First TestFlight release.

- Voice chat in Steam group voice channels, over a direct Steam connection.
- Text chat with friends and groups: history, unread badges, emoticons, stickers and photos.
- Store, Releases and Feed tabs: deals, top sellers, upcoming games, wishlist, personal calendar,
  news, friends activity and sale events.
- Game, bundle, sale and news screens with VaporLens analysis.
- Sale event pages in the device's language.
- Group voice channels shown above the group's chat, and unnamed groups named after their members.
