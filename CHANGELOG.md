# Changelog

## 0.13 (2026-10-30)

- Vaporlens is open source, linked from Settings and the sign-in screen.
- Open Settings without signing in, for the ProtonDB rating and the source link.

## 0.12 (2026-10-28)

- Swipe between screenshots in the viewer, and pan around a zoomed-in one.
- Filter the Discounted list by discount and reviews, and change how it's sorted.
- Tap the open tab again to scroll its list back to the top.
- Count all of a game's reviews, including those from key activations, and show when a game
  has few or no reviews.
- Hide the player count on DLC pages, where Steam always reports zero.
- Hide the open-game link for non-Steam games in the chat list.

## 0.11 (2026-10-27)

- Show how many people are playing a game right now on its page.
- Pull down on a game page to refresh it.
- Show how many days are left before a game comes out, if it's due within 99 days.
- Show when VaporLens last analyzed a game, and request a new analysis once that's a week old.
- Show a game's ProtonDB rating on its page. Turn it on in Settings.
- Open search in lists and chat from a button in the top bar instead of pulling down.
- Tap a game heading in the chat list to open its page.
- Show the nicknames you've set for friends in chat, and find friends by nickname in search.
- Make it clear that signing in takes your Steam account name and password.

## 0.10 (2026-10-24)

- Show a splash screen while the app starts.
- Fix the app crashing on Android when someone leaves a voice chat and joins again.
- Run on iPad as an iPhone app.

## 0.9 (2026-10-24)

- Ignore a game as played on another platform, or as not interested.
- Show wishlist, follow and ignore as outlined buttons with icons.
- Play YouTube videos inside news posts, and show games a post links to as cards. Links typed
  into posts and comments are tappable.
- Include news from games you follow or have wishlisted in News.
- Keep the selected pill in view when switching lists in Store.
- Fix game reviews not loading after Steam switched off its old reviews API.
- Fix comment and review authors showing as "Steam user".

## 0.8 (2026-10-23)

- Sign in to VaporLens with your Steam account: see how well a game fits your taste, request
  analysis of games it hasn't covered, and import your library and wishlist from Settings.
- Show games VaporLens finds similar first in "More like this".
- Open a game's editions and bundles as their own pages, from the new DLC & editions tab.
- Share a game's store link. Games you own show "In library" in place of the wishlist button.
- Hide games you own or have wishlisted from the store's lists, in Settings.
- Search within a hub or tag, and find tags by name in Browse.
- Show chats with unread messages in an Unread section on top, and group friends by the game
  they're playing.
- Mute a game's news next to its link in a post. Settings no longer lists muted news.

## 0.7 (2026-10-22)

- Mention people in group chats, see link previews, and share games with `/store`.
- Format messages, send spoiler photos and camera shots, and search a chat.
- Pick where voice plays, including headsets, and set each person's volume.
- Browse the store by hub and tag, and filter your wishlist by category.
- Read comments on news posts and mute a game's news.
- Add more Steam accounts and switch between them.

## 0.6 (2026-10-17)

- Get alerts for messages and calls while the app is in the background, and choose per chat which
  ones alert. On Android, "Stay connected" in the new Settings screen keeps them coming.
- Ring on incoming calls, with a short beep instead while you're in voice.
- Add Steam's notification inbox behind a bell.
- Keep unread messages in sync with Steam, mark chats read on your other devices too, and show a
  dot on the Chat tab while anything is unread.
- Show what friends are playing and when they're away, and set your own status in Settings.
- Open links in messages, react to messages and see when friends are typing.
- Switch between a group's rooms and see its members. Muted rooms no longer show as unread.
- Wishlist, follow and ignore games from their store page.
- Show a game's editions, DLC, system requirements, Early Access notes and similar games.
- Show playtime and when you last played on My games cards.
- Fix voice not playing through Bluetooth headsets on Android.

## 0.5 (2026-10-15)

- Split the game page into Overview, Media, About, Reviews and News tabs.
- Show a game's Steam reviews, most helpful first, and its news, updates and events.
- List a game's features, like single-player or controller support, on its page.
- Show "Coming soon" with the release date or window for games that aren't out yet.

## 0.4.3 (2026-10-15)

- Fix Steam Guard login failing on Android when approving in the Steam app on the same phone.

## 0.4.2 (2026-10-15)

- Keep the search bar hidden while lists load and when switching sub-tabs.

## 0.4.1 (2026-10-14)

- Fix missing images for newer games in the My games list.

## 0.4 (2026-10-14)

- Use the app without signing in: it opens in the store, and account lists and chat offer to sign in.
- Add a My games list to the Store tab, with recently played games first.
- Add a pull-down search bar to the store and release lists. It searches the whole store, or filters
  the wishlist and library by name.
- Mark owned and wishlisted games on store cards and game pages.
- Use native screen transitions, swipe-back and back handling.
- Make the app smoother on older Android phones.
- Fix the Android package ID for Google Play.

## 0.3 (2026-10-08)

- Add Android support: background voice calls, back navigation, zoom, icons and signed release builds.
- Load more of every store, release and calendar list, and older chat messages, while scrolling.
- Go back from pushed screens by swiping from the left edge on iOS.

## 0.2 (2026-10-07)

- Open on cached data and refresh it from Steam in the background.
- Show store games in a two-column grid with prices on the art.
- Redesign the game page after VaporLens's own report layout.
- Play game trailers fullscreen and list all media in a two-column page.
- Stop requesting own and friends' status, which signed other Steam clients out of chat and emptied
  their friends lists.

## 0.1 (2026-10-06)

First TestFlight release.

- Voice chat in Steam group voice channels, over a direct Steam connection.
- Text chat with friends and groups: history, unread badges, emoticons, stickers and photos.
- Store, Releases and Feed tabs: deals, top sellers, upcoming games, wishlist, personal calendar,
  news, friends activity and sale events.
- Game, bundle, sale and news screens with VaporLens analysis.
- Sale event pages in the device's language.
- Group voice channels shown above the group's chat, and unnamed groups named after their members.
